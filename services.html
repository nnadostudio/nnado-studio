Brand packages

Four flexible starting points for businesses at different stages. Final scope and pricing are confirmed after your inquiry.

01

Focused + foundational

Logo Essentials

Starting at $600
Best for

New or small businesses that need a polished, versatile logo foundation without a full-scale brand build.

Typically includes
  • Brand questionnaire
  • One creative direction
  • Primary logo + alternate mark
  • Core color palette
  • Typography direction

What this means+

Creative direction is the overall visual route for your brand—its mood, references, and personality. An alternate mark is a simplified logo variation for smaller spaces. Typography direction means the fonts and basic rules that keep your brand looking consistent.

Your final file suite includes the professional logo formats you’ll need for web, social, and print, so you’re not left wondering which file to use.

Scope note This package creates a new logo foundation, but does not include a full brand strategy, detailed brand guide, or designed collateral. Anything beyond the listed deliverables is quoted before it’s added.

Mini Brand

Typically includes

What this means+

A complete logo suite gives you a primary logo, secondary version, and smaller mark for different spaces. The color + typography system sets the exact colors and fonts your brand should use. A custom graphic element might be a pattern, icon, texture, or shape that makes the identity feel more ownable.

The mini brand guide puts the key rules in one reference PDF. One branded application means one designed item in one format—like a business card, one-page menu, social template, or label for one product.

the practical stuff

Website FAQ

Do I need to already have a domain?
Nope. I can help you determine what you need for your domain, hosting, and launch setup.
Will I be able to update the website myself?
Whenever possible, the site will be built so everyday content updates can be handled without touching code. The exact editing workflow depends on the platform used for the project.
Can you connect forms, scheduling, or payments?
Yes. Basic third-party integrations such as inquiry forms, scheduling tools, email signup forms, and supported payment or invoicing tools can be incorporated depending on the platform and project scope.
Do you create online stores?
Small e-commerce projects can be quoted separately depending on the number of products and required functionality.
Do you offer website maintenance?
Yes. Ongoing website updates and maintenance can be added as a monthly retainer.

Scope note Advanced custom software development, complex databases, custom web applications, and enterprise-level engineering are not included. If your project requires functionality outside the studio’s scope, additional development may be quoted separately.
