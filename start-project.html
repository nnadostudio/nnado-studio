How it works

No surprise invoices, mystery steps, or instant-booking pressure. A project becomes official only after we agree on the scope and the deposit is paid.

01 / Inquiry

You send the project details.

02 / Scope

I review the project and confirm deliverables, timeline, and final pricing.

03 / Proposal

You receive the official project scope, terms, and agreement.

04 / Deposit

The required initial payment secures the project.

05 / Kickoff

You receive onboarding materials and schedule a kickoff call if one is needed.

06 / Design

The project officially begins.
