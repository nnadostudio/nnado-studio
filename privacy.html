Privacy policy.

nnado studio respects your privacy and only asks for information reasonably needed to answer inquiries, discuss projects, provide services, and maintain basic business records.

Information collected

If you submit a project inquiry, you may provide your name, email address, business or brand name, project type, budget range, timeline, referral source, and details you choose to include. Email correspondence may also be retained as part of normal business records.

How information is used

Your information is used to respond to you, evaluate project fit, prepare proposals or agreements, deliver requested services, maintain project records, and meet legitimate legal or accounting obligations. nnado studio does not sell personal information.

Third-party services

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Inquiry forms are processed through FormSubmit when you choose to submit them. Google Fonts may be requested from Google when pages load. Those providers may process technical information under their own privacy practices.

Data minimization and retention

Only information relevant to an inquiry or client relationship is requested. Records are kept only as long as reasonably useful for communication, project administration, recordkeeping, dispute prevention, or legal obligations.

Your choices

You may email nnado studio instead of using the website form. You may also contact nnado studio to ask about personal information you submitted, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to nnadostudio@gmail.com. nnado studio is operated by Donna Graves, an independent graphic designer based in Dallas, Georgia / Metro Atlanta.
